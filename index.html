Bipolar scaling maps −100 % of the signal range to Scaling min and +100 % to Scaling max. Type 9 carries only the share of the signal range, so the master applies its own scaling. IV is set on a live-zero input below 3.6 mA or above 21 mA (NAMUR NE 43); OV is set when the signal leaves its nominal range. On bipolar and unipolar inputs a broken wire reads as a valid 0 mA.
